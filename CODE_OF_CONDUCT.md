# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any other behavior that is inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and activities where participants represent the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and appropriate action will be taken.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to violations, including warnings, removal of inappropriate content, or restricting participation in the project.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community Code of Conduct practices.
